# Legal and Operational Gates

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.

Before public token issuance, public token sale, exchange/listing activity, custody, or offering financial services, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and tax advice.

South Africa-specific items to verify at launch:
- whether the activity falls within the FSCA's CASP licensing perimeter;
- whether the business creates reporting obligations under CARF;
- income tax/CGT treatment and record-keeping;
- consumer, marketing and data-protection obligations.

Do not automate representations that an asset is an investment, promises returns, or is approved/licensed unless independently verified and legally cleared.
