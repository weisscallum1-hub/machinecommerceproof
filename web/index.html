Built with clear boundaries

Helpful intelligence, accountable decisions.

What does the snapshot check?

Public website availability and content signals, common metadata and structured data, machine-readable business information, published policy pages, and visible baseline security headers. AI visibility is a framework for evidence and manual assessment, not a claim that a model recommends you.

Does it test for vulnerabilities?

No. It is a passive review of public responses only. It does not probe vulnerabilities, enumerate services, bypass controls or access private systems.

Can AgentReady change my website or send messages?

No automated production changes or outbound outreach are enabled. Recommendations and drafts remain review-only; consequential actions require your explicit approval.

How should I interpret the score?

It is a prioritization aid based on observable signals, not a guarantee of safety, search placement, revenue or transaction success. Missing evidence is not proof of a failure.
